Homemade Recipe: Granola & Yogurt

A delicious, healthy, and quick snack or breakfast for any day!

Why this recipe is special to me..

It is one of the best things I have ever eaten. My mom had bought a cup of it and wanted me to try it, and once I did, I fell in love with it instantly. We always enjoy eating it together. It was so good that we started making it at home.

Ingredients
  • ½ cup Granola
  • ¼ cup Greek Yogurt
  • ½ cup Unsweetened Almond Milk
  • 1-3 tsp Chia seeds
  • 1 tbsp Maple Syrup or Honey

Nutrition
To make this yummy and nutritious protein yogurt breakfast or snack even healthier, ensure the granola is low on sugar, use plain or nonfat Greek yogurt, and reduce the amount of honey or maple syrup.
  • Calories: 350-450 kcal
  • Protein: 15-25 grams
  • Fiber: 8-15 grams
  • Carbohydrates: 40-55 grams
  • Total Fat: 15-20 grams
  • Sugar: 10-15 grams
